Computed stability labels did not help nanobody melting-temperature prediction
equally. With a frozen sequence encoder, MD native-contact labels from local
mutation scans gave a larger MAE reduction than labels from a
heterogeneous structure panel. The protocols and model searches also differed,
so the contrast cannot be assigned to variant choice alone. FEP mutation free
energies gave the lowest test error with both frozen and fine-tuned
encoders, although the 95% interval for the fine-tuned change included zero.
